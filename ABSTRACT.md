# Abstract

This project analyzes retail sales data from a U.S.-based retail store to uncover key insights and trends driving business performance. The analysis used data from sales orders, customers, products, regions, and state-level regions, processed through an ETL pipeline using SQL to ensure data consistency and integrity. An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) was conducted entirely in Python, focusing on revenue distribution, profit margins, and performance patterns across regions and customer segments. Following the EDA, an interactive Power BI dashboard was built to visualize sales, profits, and regional trends dynamically.

The analysis shows that overall profitability is stable, with an average profit margin around 37%. High-revenue states such as California, Illinois, Florida, and Texas generate the largest sales volumes, while smaller states like Nebraska and Wyoming achieve higher margins, demonstrating efficiency at lower scale. At the product level, items such as the Aspen Leather Backpack and Montclair Leather Tote stand out for combining solid margins (39–40%) with high revenue, reflecting a balanced strategy between pricing and demand. A time series model using Prophet was developed to forecast monthly revenue from March to December 2018 based on historical sales from January 2014 to February 2018, predicting peaks in March, November, and December that reflect typical retail seasonal cycles.

It is recommended that further analysis be conducted to obtain deeper product-level insights, including predicting demand for individual items to support sales planning, inventory optimization, and strategic stock management. Additional recommended analyses include evaluating the effects of seasonal patterns and customer segmentation to enhance operational decision-making and overall efficiency in the retail business.
